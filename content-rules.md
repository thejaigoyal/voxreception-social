# VoxReception content engine: facts and rules

The automated content engine may only state what is listed under "Allowed facts". The quality check rejects any post that breaks a rule under "Banned". Edit this file to change what the system says.

## Allowed facts (verified from the product, not from site placeholders)
- VoxReception is an AI voice receptionist. It answers inbound calls and places outbound calls.
- Channels: phone, web chat, web voice widget, WhatsApp, email.
- It books, reschedules and confirms appointments, answers FAQs, takes caller details, forwards or warm-transfers calls to a human.
- Outbound: upload a list, describe the conversation, and it calls (reminders, confirmations, lead qualification, follow-ups).
- Live demo line anyone can call: +1 (859) 724-5646 (US/Canada). Primary call to action.
- Pricing: Professional $350/mo (500 min), Business $1,490/mo (3,000 min), Scale custom.
- Website: voxreception.com. Contact: hello@voxreception.com.
- TO CONFIRM with Vikas before use: languages supported, CRM/calendar integrations that are live today, response latency, free-trial terms, go-live time.

## Banned
- Any customer count, call volume, uptime, conversion, savings or ROI number (e.g. "12M+ calls", "99.99%", "+38%").
- Testimonials, quotes, customer names, logos or case studies, until real customers exist and agree in writing.
- Compliance claims (SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, BAA) until certification is in hand.
- Saying it is "better than" a named competitor.
- Implying a human answers; posts may say callers often can't tell, never that it is a person.
- Promising medical, legal or financial outcomes.
- Encouraging cold robocalls; outbound content must mention consent and opt-outs.

## Voice
- Plain, confident, a little playful. Short sentences. No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changer").
- Speak to small business owners who miss calls: clinics, salons, home services, law offices.
- Every post ends with one call to action: call the demo line, or visit voxreception.com.

## Platform formats
- Instagram: carousel or Reel, caption under 150 words, 3 to 5 hashtags.
- Facebook: same asset as Instagram, caption can be longer.
- X: under 270 characters, no hashtag spam (max 1).
- YouTube: Shorts under 60 seconds built from demo-line call clips; title under 70 characters.
- Blog: 800 to 1,500 words, one target search phrase, links to the demo line.

## Safety switches
- Kill switch: a single setting stops all publishing.
- Daily summary to Vikas of everything published (information only).
- Any post the quality check rejects twice is dropped, not retried forever.
